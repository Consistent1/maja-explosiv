Concept and design of the railing with three ghostlike festival visitors for the terrace on the new building extension of the Tubebox Hangar, commissioned by Fusion Festival. Realization of the 50 meter of swirled rail and the 3 statues together with Horst Steel /D, KAI /D and Uri Moss /IL, June 2013, Lärz, Germany.
