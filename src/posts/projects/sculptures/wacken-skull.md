Concept and realization of the entrance gate to the VIP Area at Wacken Open Air festival. The design evolved from the established festival logo.

The light-duty sculpture is demountable into 4 pieces, the body is shaped in Styrofoam, with a supporting inner metal structure and coated with Acrystal. Dimensions : 6,5m high x 8m wide

Planned and constructed together with Hannes Jung /D and Erico Moreira /BRA, in BBK Werkstätten, Berlin, March 2012.
