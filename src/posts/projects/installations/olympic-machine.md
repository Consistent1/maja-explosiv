The Olympic Machine is based on the articulated, electric train arm design. It is a simple weight-shifting system for acrobatic performances. Concept and construction together with Marco Collabucci /I. Realized in the context of 'Wheel of Power', a theatrical production in an empty train factory, staged by Derevo /RU for the 400th anniversary of the city of Mannheim /D, 2007
