Design and realization of the metal statue of Bill Parooka, the new face and image
of the mayor and founder of the festival town Parookaville.
Including his pedestal, the monument of Bill reaches an overall size of 7,20 m.
Built in commission for the Parookaville Festival on Weeze airport, Germany.

The sculpture was realized in only four weeks with the following stunning crew:
Hannes Jung - Atlantis SFX IRL/D, Jens Schendel a.k.a Horst Steel /D,
Uri Moss D/IL, Marco Colabucci - Supernova B/I, Floris De Groot -
Nebelbrigade D/NL and Seb Lamparsky /D
The project was facilitated thanks to the workshop and general great support of
the Sisyphos Club in Berlin, June 2016
