During the action time of one week, a 3x6 m HIV writing was destroyed, while an
angel-like figure grew out of the debris.

The campaign 'Zerstöre HIV' was initiated for the World AIDS Day 2013.
It was entirely designed to be presented to, and interactively controlled by the
audience on social media. Concept by Jugend gegen Aids and Deepblue Networks, Hamburg
Realisation with Horst Steel /D, KAI /D and Uri Moss /IL, November 2013, Alte Münze,
Berlin /D
