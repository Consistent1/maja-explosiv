Portrait series of three artists I admire and which surely have influenced my artistic
way and development. In this work I concentrate on the face and facial expression while
searching for possibilities to work spontaneously and sketchily. In order of creation: Alberto Giacometti, 1911 - 1966, Swiss sculptor, painter, draftsman and printmaker Käthe Kollwitz, 1867 - 1945, German draftswoman and printmaker Bernhard Luginbühl, 1929 - 2011, Swiss iron sculptor, printmaker and draftsman
