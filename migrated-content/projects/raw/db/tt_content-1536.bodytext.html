Concept and design of the 2,5 ton sculpture for the new 'Thrash of the Titans Field'
at Wacken Open Air 2012. The Wacken Bull is entirely built from scrap metal and was realized in 12 days
on location with excellent teamwork from Hannes Jung, Marco Colabucci, Thomas Richter
and Jens Schendel a.k.a. Horst Steel.
The construction time included the days of the festival, during which time a live workshop
was conducted.

This project was facilitated thanks to the major support of the W-O-A Organization,
as well as a stunning collaboration with the scrapyard ISR Itzehoe.
