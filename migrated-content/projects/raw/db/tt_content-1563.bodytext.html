Realization of the rough and sculpture-like furniture for the 'Tacheles scene' in the
thriller based on real events around Julian Assange and Daniel Domscheit-Berg,
directed by Bill Condon. The sitting area, consisting of 4 chairs, 1 sofa, 1 table and 6 lamps was realised
together with KAI /D and Stahlrichter /D, January 2013, Berlin, Germany.
