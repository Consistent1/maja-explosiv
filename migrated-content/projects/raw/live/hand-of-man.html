Hand of Man, 2007

Invited by Robochrist Industries /USA, Maja Thommen assisted in the building of this large
scale sculpture which was constructed in only three weeks for the 10th Robodock Festival, 2007.
For the premiere of this incredible machine, the 'Hand' crushed a minivan.
