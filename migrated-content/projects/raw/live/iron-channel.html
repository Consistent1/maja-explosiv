The Iron Channel, 1995

This first largescale group work of XIX was realized in the context of the multimedia event
Eurokot. 7 tons of scrap were welded into a 10 meters long installation, including sound
machines and light effects, in one arm of the pedestrian underpass Escherwyss Unterführung,
Zurich /CH
