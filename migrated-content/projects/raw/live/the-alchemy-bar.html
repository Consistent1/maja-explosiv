The Alchemy Bar, 2006

The conception, design and realisation of a 24m long, kinetic bar counter, commissioned
by the Robodock Festival, Amsterdam in 2006.
Based on the four elements theory, the counter is divided into four self-standing bars
with the themes water, earth, fire and air. The bar had to be constructed from recycled
material and is designed to be collapsible.

Rent the Alchemy Bar!
