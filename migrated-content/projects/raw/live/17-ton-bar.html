The 17 ton Bar, 1997

The base for this massive bar was made from the recycled, one hundred year old, steel
girders of gate 17 of Zurich's main train station.
The 17 ton Bar was installed at the Zoom Festival and was accompanied by a mad 'art car'
and pyrotechnics performance in 1997.
