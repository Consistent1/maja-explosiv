Bagger, 1997-2001

Bagger was a combative stage show formed around the noise art performance of
a live crust-core punk band. This decidedly non- commercial project combined music,
theatre, acrobatics and fire art. The shows elaborate costumes and set pieces made
from tyres, rubber, and scrap metal were constructed entirely of recycled waste materials.
The Parisien video collective Fist Fuck Productions provided additional visuals by way of
Super 8 projections.

During the four years that Bagger was together, they toured extensively throughout
Europe including shows in Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Italy, Croatia,
Slovenia, Greece and Sweden.

The Swiss photographer Kati Bitzer made a documentary book about the Bagger tours.
