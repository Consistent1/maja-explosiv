Derevo, Wheel of Power, 2007

For the 400th anniversary of the city of Mannheim, Theatrical masters Derevo /RU produced
a full evening of spectacular with AKHE theatre from St. Petersburg and a collaboration of
installation artist called Robodock Crew based around Tom van der Stelt /NL.

For their part, the Robodock Crew designed and animated the outdoor space of an abandoned
railway station with bars, sculptures, machines and various fire performances.

The Robodock crew was: Tom van der Stelt /NL, Daan /NL, Doyle /USA, Jens Schendel a.k.a.
Horst Steel /D, Luca Minotti /CH, Martin Cypritch /CZ, Marco Collabucci /I, Wout Rockx /NL,
Ymar /NL, Rael /NL, Maja Explosiv /CH
