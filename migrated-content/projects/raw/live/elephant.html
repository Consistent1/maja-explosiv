Elephants Head

Realization of the 8 meter elephants head for the for the large scale Bollywood show India, the follower of
Afrika-Afrika, together with Tom Stelt /NL, Ymar Degroot /NL and Martin Cypritch /SK, commissioned by
Prime Time Entertainment, Mainz /D, November 2009

The sculpture is made from 16 pieces, weighs less then 120 kg and is designed to be built on stage for the
gran finale of the show.
