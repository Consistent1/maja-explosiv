4.5 by 2.0m wall sculpture for the dining room of the Kulturkosmos guesthouse, Fusion Festival, Lärz, MV. The inspiration for the harbor scene is based on the history of the house, which was built as an apprentice house for the Rechlin shipyard. The drawing also refers to the ship's room, das Schiffszimmer, where a different, smaller view of the cranes is depicted. And which we could decorate and furnish for the guesthouse in 2022. The metal drawing was realized in the Atelier Thommen & Moss in Berlin and could be installed in Lärz in May 2024
