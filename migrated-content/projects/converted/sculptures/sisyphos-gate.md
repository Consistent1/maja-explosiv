Concept and design of the main gate for the Sisyphos Club. The gateway with the two slightly three dimensional kissing ducks spans over 6 by 6 meters. In the ribbon above the gate five DMX controlled propan gas flamethrowers are incorporated. Realization of the precision metalwork on site in great teamwork with: Horst Steel /D, KAI /D, Erico Moreira /BRA and Uri Moss /IL. The fire installation was realized by Eddie Egal /D

August 2013, Berlin, Germany
