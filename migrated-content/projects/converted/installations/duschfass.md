Fire-shower installation for the wellness area of Bar 25 Concept and realization with Bastiaan Maris /NL and Eddie Egal /D, Berlin, June 2010 In the wooden barrel we installed one all around shower from six shower heads and one flood shower from 4 individual toilet tanks, which, when the water gets released, trigger two flame throwers above the barrel
