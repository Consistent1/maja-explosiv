Custom fencing for the 'Apocalyptic Zen Beer Garden' of the new White Trash Fast Food restaurant in Berlin Treptow, embedding one fully stripped coach and three upright standing car wrecks, May 2014.

Concept by Wally, White Trash Fast Food. Design and realisation together with Horst Steel, Stahlrichter, Marco Schöne and Uri Moss. Flamethrowers by Eddie Egal.
