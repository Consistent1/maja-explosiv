2024
Die grosse Hafensszene The 4,5 x 2m metal drawing of the bigger harbor scene, for the dining room of Seminarhaus Kulturkosmos, Larz, North Germany.

Wandskulptur Hinwil The 8,5 x 3m iron drawing pictures the town of Hinwil. Realized for the local branch of the Raiffeisen Bank in Hinwil.

Das Schiffszimmer Overall design of a hotel room, including a metal wall picture of a harbor scene, Seminarhaus Kulturkosmos, Larz, North Germany.
2021
Die Blumenwolke A custom design chandelier from over 280 cutlery flowers for a lofty home in Switzerland.
2020
Die Affenbande A monkey gang of seven projected to climb over a private residence in Zurich. In September 2022 five monkeys are realized and installed.
2019
Flower Power Two large flower bouquets, realised from over 1800 pieces of cutlery, commissioned by Monster Ronson's Ichiban Karaoke, Berlin /D.

The Wolf Life size sculpture of a male wolf, work in commission. Berlin /D.
2018
The Raven and the Crow Two separate commissions of birds, following the exposition in Switzerland, Berlin /D.

Kunst ist Leben Solo exhibition, Museum Eva Wipf, Pfaffikon Zurich /CH.
2018
Series of 3 metal portraits Alberto Giacometti, Kathe Kollwitz and Bernhard Luginbuhl, Berlin /D.

DNA Helixes Two variations of turning DNA Helixes, for the sculpture garden at the Sisyphos Club, Berlin /D.
2017
Die Weglampen A path illumination with 15 different lamps for the climbing refuge Kalandraka in Rodellar /E. Built with the metal group XIX in Sennhof /CH.

Tattling Bird Bird garden sculpture on commission, Berlin /D.

The Lionfish Door The custom designed garden door was realized in Sennhof /CH.
